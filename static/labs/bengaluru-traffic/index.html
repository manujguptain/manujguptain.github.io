Bengaluru Traffic-Risk Calendar

Find the quieter and busier times to travel in your part of Bengaluru. We start with historical Bengaluru traffic patterns by weekday and time, then account for holidays, long weekends, rain and known disruptions.

No Google traffic data. Historical traffic backbone uses public TomTom Traffic Index patterns; this is not a route ETA.

How to use this

First: choose the quieter timeEach time window shows its typical historical traffic level and an approximate minutes-per-10-km figure. A 6–8 AM window can therefore be clearly better than 8–10 AM even when both are completely normal days.
Then: check what is different todayPublic holidays, restricted holidays, school closures, long weekends and weather are treated separately rather than calling every festival a citywide holiday.
Long weekends change several daysDepartures can worsen traffic before the break, city traffic can ease while people are away, and return traffic can build on Sunday evening and sometimes Monday morning.

TomTom's public Bengaluru 2025 citywide weekday/hour travel patterns provide the historical backbone. Broad area adjustments are estimates, not measured TomTom zone-level data. Weather, roadworks, rallies and events become clearer closer to the date.
