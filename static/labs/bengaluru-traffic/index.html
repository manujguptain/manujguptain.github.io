Bengaluru Traffic-Risk Calendar

See when traffic may be lighter or heavier than usual in your part of Bengaluru, and the simple reason why — holidays, long weekends, rain and other known disruptions.

No Google traffic data. This predicts traffic pressure, not exact travel time.

How to use this

Look for the percentage“20% lighter” means we expect less traffic pressure than is usual for that same weekday and time.
Holiday type mattersA statewide public holiday, a restricted holiday and a school-only closure are treated differently. We do not assume Bengaluru is shut just because a festival appears on a calendar.
Long weekends have a patternTraffic can rise before people leave, fall while they are away, and rise again on Sunday evening or Monday morning when they return.

These are experimental estimates, not navigation instructions or exact journey times. Weather, roadworks, rallies and events become clearer closer to the day.
