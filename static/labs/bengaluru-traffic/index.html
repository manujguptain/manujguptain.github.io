Bengaluru Traffic-Risk Calendar

Quickly see when Bengaluru roads are likely to be easier or harder to travel, and why.

POC only. Experimental research demo, not an official traffic forecast or navigation service. No Google traffic data is used.
About this experiment

This non-commercial proof of concept explores what can be derived from publicly available traffic patterns and public information. Source owners retain rights in their original material. Estimates may be incomplete or wrong.

How to read it

Traffic levelThe 5-step scale tells you how busy the roads are expected to be for that time window. It is not the percentage change.
Lighter or heavier than usualThe separate percentage compares that date with a normal same weekday and time. For example, “6% lighter than usual” means only a modest improvement.
WhyThe reason is always shown—holiday, school closure, long-weekend effect, rain or an area rush-hour pattern.

Historical backbone: public TomTom Traffic Index Bengaluru patterns. Area context: Bengaluru mobility plans and published corridor/junction studies. Weather and other short-term signals become clearer closer to the date.

Sources used in this POC

These links are provided for transparency. The POC derives small signals and summaries rather than republishing the source datasets.

  • TomTom Traffic Index — Bengaluru — citywide historical weekday/hour traffic pattern.
  • Bengaluru Comprehensive Mobility Plan / OpenCity — junction and corridor context.
  • Open-Meteo — near-term weather signals.
  • Bengaluru Traffic Police — traffic advisories and public traffic information.
  • Government of Karnataka — Varthe — official state notices/news used for verification where available.
