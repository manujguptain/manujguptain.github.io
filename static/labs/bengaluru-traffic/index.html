Bengaluru Traffic-Risk Calendar

Find the quieter and busier times to travel in your part of Bengaluru. We start with historical Bengaluru traffic patterns by weekday and time, then account for holidays, long weekends, rain and known disruptions.

No Google traffic data. Historical traffic backbone uses public TomTom Traffic Index patterns; this is not a route ETA.

How to use this

Look at the expected traffic firstThe main label combines the historical pattern with known effects for that date. The normal weekday/time value is shown only as a reference.
Holiday effects start before the holidayThe evening before any statewide public holiday can become busier because of early departures, shopping and intercity travel—not only before Friday holidays.
Long weekends affect several daysTraffic can rise before people leave, ease while they are away, and rise again on Sunday evening or Monday morning as they return.

TomTom's public Bengaluru 2025 citywide weekday/hour travel patterns provide the historical backbone. Area-specific changes are only applied when we have area-specific evidence; we do not invent zone travel-time multipliers.
