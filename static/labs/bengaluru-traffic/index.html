Bengaluru Traffic-Risk Calendar

An experimental proof of concept exploring whether public historical traffic patterns, holidays, long weekends, weather and disruption signals can help people choose better travel times in Bengaluru.

POC only. This is a research/experimentation demo, not a navigation service or official traffic forecast. No Google traffic data is used.
About this experiment

This page is a non-commercial ManujG AI Labs proof of concept created to explore what is possible with publicly available information. It summarizes and derives signals from referenced sources rather than reproducing their datasets as a competing service. Source owners retain rights in their original material. Estimates can be incomplete or wrong and should not be treated as official traffic advice.

How to use this

Look at the expected traffic firstThe main label combines Bengaluru's historical weekday/time pattern with known effects for that date. The normal value is shown underneath as a reference.
Holiday effects start before the holidayThe evening before a statewide public holiday can become busier because of early departures, shopping and intercity travel.
Area peaks can differWhere published studies provide local peak timings, those windows are highlighted. Otherwise the Bengaluru-wide timing curve is used rather than inventing a local pattern.

Historical backbone: public TomTom Traffic Index Bengaluru patterns. Area peak context: Bengaluru Comprehensive Mobility Plan and related published corridor/junction studies. Weather, roadworks, rallies and events become clearer closer to the date.
