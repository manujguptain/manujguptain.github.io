Bengaluru Traffic-Risk Calendar

Plan around unusually difficult traffic before you leave. The baseline uses Bengaluru time patterns, zone behaviour and known holidays. Weather, roadworks, rallies and major events will be added as fresh intelligence becomes available.

No Google traffic data. This is a risk forecast, not a route ETA.

How to read this

RiskExpected congestion pressure for that zone and time window.
ConfidenceHow much evidence is currently available. Long-range dates are intentionally lower confidence.
Traffic shadowHolidays can alter behaviour before and after the holiday, including outbound and return traffic.

Baseline forecasts are experimental and should not be used as navigation instructions. Predictions will become more specific as verified weather, roadwork, rally, strike and major-event signals are integrated.
