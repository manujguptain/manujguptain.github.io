Bengaluru Traffic-Risk Calendar

See when traffic may be lighter or heavier than usual in your part of Bengaluru, and the simple reason why — holidays, rain and other known disruptions.

No Google traffic data. This predicts traffic pressure, not exact travel time.

How to use this

Look for the percentage“30% lighter” means we expect less traffic pressure than is usual for that same day and time.
Read the reasonWe explain the main known cause in ordinary language, such as a holiday or expected rain.
Far-away dates can changeHoliday patterns are known early. Weather, roadworks, rallies and other events become clearer closer to the day.

These are experimental estimates, not navigation instructions or exact journey times.
