Which parts of Bengaluru look active, pressured or unclear?

This page turns public market research into a corridor-level screening view. It is designed to help a buyer decide what to investigate next—not to predict a property price or pretend that research estimates are registration records.

City answer

Active, but supply is expanding faster than demand.

Sales are higher than last year, but the latest quarter shows moderation and inventory pressure is uneven. The city headline is therefore incomplete: the corridor and project matter.

Evidence and limitations

What this does—and does not—measure

Sales and launches here are publisher-reported primary-market estimates. They are not legal sale-deed registrations. We do not combine incompatible publisher numbers into one fake total. West and Central have insufficient comparable public data, so the page deliberately says so.
