Cross-source interpretation

What can we reasonably say?

Demand is material

JLL reports approximately 17,000 Bengaluru units of demand in Q2 2026, but this is a research estimate—not a registration count.

Launches slowed

JLL reported 27,055 launches in Q1 2026, while Cushman reported 12,544 in Q2. These are different publishers, so the direction is informative but not a perfect like-for-like series.

Supply may be ahead

The available evidence shows substantial new supply relative to reported demand, but it is not enough to calculate true absorption without consistent quarterly sales and inventory data.

Current verdict: active but not yet measurable with confidence

The useful conclusion is not “prices will rise.” It is that Bengaluru has meaningful primary-market activity, large launch volumes and insufficiently consistent public data for locality-level purchase conclusions.
