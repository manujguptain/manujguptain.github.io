City-Level Synthesis

Active, but new supply expansion is outpacing sales absorption.

Overall sales volume and developer launches have expanded robustly compared to last year. However, new launches surged 71% YoY in Q2 2026 while demand cooled 6% quarter-on-quarter, driving citywide unsold inventory up 22% YoY. Aggregate city headlines obscure stark corridor divergence: negotiation room is high in South Bengaluru, while prime North and tech East retain tight absorption.

Actionable Framework

How to Translate Market Signals Into a Purchase Decision

South Corridor

Negotiate on Ready Stock

With 7.4 quarters of inventory overhang, buyers hold strong cards. Ask for base price concessions, staggered payment schedules, and waiver of floor rise and premium charges.

East Corridor

Scrutinize Water & Infrastructure

High launch volumes mean you have ample choices. Filter projects strictly by confirmed BWSSB Cauvery water pipeline connections, approach road access, and builder delivery track record.

North Corridor

Verify Development Horizon

North exhibits the lowest overhang (3.9 QTS) and strong capital appreciation. However, outer aerospace/KIADB hubs require a 5–7 year horizon; verify social infrastructure before paying high premiums.

Methodology Boundaries

What This Measures — And Does Not Measure

All sales, launch, and inventory counts shown are publisher-reported primary-market research estimates. They are not legal registered sale deeds from the Kaveri Sub-Registrar offices. We never combine incompatible surveys into a synthetic single total. Where public data is thin (Central and West), it is clearly flagged as insufficient.
