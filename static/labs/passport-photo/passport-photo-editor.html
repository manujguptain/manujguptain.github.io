Major Country Requirements

🇺🇸 USA: 2×2 inches (51×51mm). White background. Head 1-1 3/8" from chin to top.
🇬🇧 UK / 🇪🇺 EU: 35×45mm. Off-white/Cream background. Face height 29-34mm.
🇮🇳 India: 2×2" or 35×45mm. White background. Recent color photo.
🇯🇵 Japan: 35×45mm. Solid background. Neutral expression.

⚠️ Disclaimer: This tool provides no guarantees for the formats for the regions. It is for the user to verify themselves against official government requirements before printing or submitting.

💡 Pro Tip: Stand against a plain white wall with good natural lighting for the best results.
