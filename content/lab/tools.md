### [SysCanvas Architecture Explorer](/tools/syscanvas/)
**Type:** Web Application | **Status:** MVP

A 100% client-side visualization tool for Adaptive AUTOSAR architectures. It is designed to make complex vehicle domains easy to read and navigate.

* **Instant Visualization:** Upload `.arxml` files to instantly map domain relationships and network connections.
* **Privacy-First:** All processing happens locally. Your architecture files never leave your browser.
* **Interactive Canvas:** Powered by D3.js for smooth, drag-and-drop exploration.
